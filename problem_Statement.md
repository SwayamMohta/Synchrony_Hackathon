Next-Gen Credit Intelligence: Building a Real-Time, Multi-Modal Underwriting Engine

Objective: To design and develop a robust, data-driven analytics solution that expands credit access to "New-to-Credit" (NTC) and "thin-file" customers by leveraging alternative data and real-time behavioral signals, while simultaneously mitigating fraud and ensuring regulatory transparency.

The Challenge:
Traditional credit models often rely on static historical data, which creates a barrier for millions of potentially creditworthy individuals who lack formal banking footprints.
In a fast-evolving BFSI landscape, the goal is to shift from reactive scoring to proactive, contextual credit decisioning.
